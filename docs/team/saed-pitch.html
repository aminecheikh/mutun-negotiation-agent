03 · How it is built

Code prices. The model reads and writes.
A guard decides what leaves.

model · ~4 sWritethe e-mail around fixed sentences
codeGuardmust-haves, C, known IDs, no first-offer accept

60 s per move, 10 cases at once

Two model calls per round: median ~8 s, worst case under 23 s in our parallel bench.

Every model call has a rule fallback

Late, malformed or unsafe answer → the rule-based move, never a lost case.

Nothing supplier-specific in code

Final suppliers are new; rules come from behaviour, not names.

04 · Trust is the currency

Every sentence is one we can back.

“The delivery record figures you cite do not match the records. That costs trust.” a supplier, after one wrong figure in practice

Claims are pre-verified

Checked against facts.json with the arena's own rule, at most three per message, each data ID once per case, copied word for word.

The writer is checked before sending

Rejected if it invents a figure or ID, talks about data it cannot cite, claims a move we did not make, reveals our private notes (“your bank”, “idle shifts”), or leaks urgency or a budget.
